01 — The problem

Every study launch waited on a person.

Reputable Health runs real-world-evidence studies — validating health and wellness products with wearables like Oura, Apple Watch and Whoop. But every new study had to be marketed and recruited for, and that meant building a recruitment landing page, visuals and ads from scratch.

It was a multi-hour job, repeated study after study, bottlenecked on whoever owned the workflow. Launches queued behind a single person — slowing the pace at which the team could put studies in the field.

02 — The approach

An AI tool that builds the whole launch.

I designed and built a tool that takes a study's brief — its protocol, audience and goals — and generates everything a launch needs to recruit participants: a recruitment landing page, on-brand imagery, and ready-to-run Facebook ads. The person running the study reviews and adjusts, instead of assembling each piece by hand.

Under the hood, GPT-4 reads the study documents to pull out the claims and target audience, an image-generation pass produces editorial visuals, and an ad generator drafts the Facebook campaigns — wired into a single app the team could run themselves.

03 — The outcome

From half a day to half an hour.

Launch setup dropped from roughly six hours to about thirty minutes — turning a recurring bottleneck into something the team could run on demand, the moment a study was ready.

A single study brief now produced the full recruitment kit — landing page, imagery and Facebook ads — so launching the next study no longer meant waiting in line behind a designer.
