Loewy / MAYA icon directions

Marks drawn from Raymond Loewy's actual work (streamliner trains, the Lucky Strike target) and from the MAYA principle itself (Most Advanced Yet Acceptable). Shown large, as a nav lockup, and at favicon size, the only test that matters for a mark. Same marker hand as the brand; cream is placeholder and would become transparent on the final.

For reference, the current mark is the Maya robot (brand/maya/favicon.svg).
