What I do

What I do

I build products. Some I keep and run myself; some I build for founders. Either way I cover the whole stack — research, design, prototyping, and shipping production code with AI tooling (Claude, Cursor).

How fractional works

When I take client work, I embed with one founder at a time — usually three to six months at 20–40 hrs/week. Strong preference for engagements that ship an actual product, not just a deliverable.

Who I work with

Technical founders building in complex, regulated spaces — healthcare especially, plus fintech, edtech, enterprise. Pre-seed to Series A. People who want an embedded product partner, not a logo.

About

I'm Humayun. I've spent the last decade designing products in complex, regulated spaces — at Shopify (pre-IPO), ClassDojo (50M+ users), Roadmunk (acq. Tempo), and Tiny Hearts (acq. Shopify), before going independent in 2020.

Studio Maya takes its name from the MAYA principle — "Most Advanced Yet Acceptable" — coined by industrial designer Raymond Loewy. Every product I build aims for that line: as advanced as the user can absorb, no further.

Based in Montreal. Work remote, anywhere.
